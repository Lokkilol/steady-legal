Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Steady is operated by Justin Wu, operating as Steady, a sole proprietor ("we," "us," or "our"). Steady provides an SMS check-in service for pickleball and tennis coaches: we send proactive check-in text messages to a coach's existing clients between coaching sessions, in the coach's name, and summarize clients' replies for the coach before the next session.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at jwu1723@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

When you use Steady, we collect:

  • Contact information: your mobile phone number and first name, provided by you or by your coach when you enroll.
  • Message content: the text messages you send and receive through Steady, including your replies to check-ins.
  • Program preferences: your chosen check-in frequency (daily, every few days, or weekly) and your opt-in and opt-out status, including the date and time you opted in or out.
  • Coach information: your coach's name and contact information, so messages can be sent in your coach's name ("[Coach Name] via Steady").
  • Service data: message delivery status and basic technical information needed to operate the service (for example, whether a message was delivered).

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you check-in text messages at the frequency you chose, and deliver messages between you and your coach.
  • Summarize your replies for your coach before your next session, so your coach has context on how your practice is going.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Steady service, including troubleshooting delivery problems.
  • Honor opt-out requests and comply with applicable law, including SMS messaging regulations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not send you marketing messages.

3. How we share your information

We share your information only as needed to provide the service:

  • Service providers: we use Twilio to deliver text messages, and an AI service provider to help generate check-in messages and summarize your replies for your coach. These providers process your information only on our instructions and only to provide their services to us.
  • Your coach: your replies to check-ins are shared with your coach in the form of a summary, as described above.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety and rights of our users.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not share your mobile phone number, opt-in status, or message content with any third party for marketing, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Your choices

  • Opt out at any time: reply STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, END, or QUIT to any Steady message. You will stop receiving messages and receive a confirmation text.
  • Get help: reply HELP, INFO, or SUPPORT to any Steady message, or email us at jwu1723@gmail.com.
  • Access or deletion: you may ask us to correct or delete your personal information by emailing jwu1723@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

5. Data retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal obligations. When you opt out, we stop sending you messages and keep only the minimum information needed to honor your opt-out (your phone number and opt-out status). You may request deletion of your information at any time by emailing jwu1723@gmail.com.

6. Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

Steady is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 18, please have a parent or guardian review this Privacy Policy and provide consent before enrolling.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will post the updated policy here with a new "Last updated" date. Your continued use of Steady after an update means you accept the updated policy.
