How Clients Opt In to Steady Text Check-Ins

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Steady sends proactive check-in text messages on behalf of coaches and tutors to their existing clients between sessions. Clients only receive messages after they have opted in. This page shows exactly how a client is invited to sign up and what the opt-in looks like on their phone.

Step 1 — The coach introduces Steady in person

After the client's first session, their coach or tutor mentions Steady face to face — no pitch, just a quick explanation. The coach says something like:

"I use a service called Steady to stay connected with my clients between sessions. It'll just text you from a phone number, like a regular text — you pick how often, daily, every few days, or weekly. You'll get a text to confirm, just reply YES if you're up for it."

Step 2 — The client receives an invite text

The client then receives an invite text on their phone from Steady's number, +1 (781) 705-7061. This is what it looks like:

Hi [Name], it's [Coach Name] via Steady — [Coach Name] would like to check in with you by text between sessions. Reply YES to start and pick how often.
YES
How often would you like check-ins? Reply 1 for daily, 2 for every few days, or 3 for weekly.
2
Welcome to [Coach Name]'s Steady check-ins! You'll get check-ins at the frequency you picked. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

The full opt-in exchange, as it appears on the client's phone.

Step 3 — The client picks a frequency and is enrolled

After replying YES, the client picks how often they want check-ins by replying 1 for daily, 2 for every few days, or 3 for weekly. They immediately receive a confirmation text with the program details, their chosen frequency, and how to get help or opt out. Only then are they enrolled — no check-in messages are sent before this exchange is complete.

Opting out: a client can stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, END, or QUIT. For help, they can reply HELP, INFO, or SUPPORT, or email jwu1723@gmail.com.
